HANDOFF FROM THE BRAND AGENT TO THE DOCS AGENT
(plain text on purpose; README/CONTRIBUTING/docs are owned by DOCS, nothing here was edited by BRAND)

1. What the shareable build is
   - Name: IslandPup, an unofficial fork of Vorssaint (GPL-3.0-or-later). Bundle: IslandPup.app
   - Bundle id: local.islandpup.app   (dev variant: "IslandPup (Developer).app", local.islandpup.app.dev)
   - Apple Silicon only (build target arm64-apple-macosx14.0), macOS 14 or later.
   - Not notarized, not signed by any Apple developer account. Signed ad-hoc ("--adhoc").
   - Fan control is not included in this build (the helper needs the maintainer's Apple team).
   - There is no auto-update. A new version means sending a new zip.
   - Screenshot/recording share links and "Send feedback" do nothing (their upstream server is
     switched off on purpose). Everything else runs locally.

2. How the builder makes the zip (the person who owns the Mac that compiles it)
   Needs only Xcode Command Line Tools.
       ./build.sh --adhoc --zip
   Output: build/stage/IslandPup.app and build/IslandPup.zip
   (use --dev --adhoc --zip for the "IslandPup (Developer)" variant; use plain ./build.sh --zip
   only if a local self-signed identity is wanted, it creates a keychain entry on that Mac).
   Rename the product in one place: APP_BASE_NAME (and BUNDLE_ID_BASE) at the top of build.sh,
   plus AppInfo.name in Sources/Vorssaint/Core/AppInfo.swift. Details of the other places are in
   the BRAND agent report.

3. How a friend opens it (exact steps to put in the README)
   a. Unzip IslandPup.zip, drag IslandPup.app into /Applications.
   b. First launch is blocked by Gatekeeper because the app is downloaded, ad-hoc signed and not
      notarized. Pick ONE of these:
      Option 1, Terminal (works on every macOS version, most reliable):
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/IslandPup.app
          open /Applications/IslandPup.app
      Option 2, right-click: Control-click (right-click) IslandPup.app, choose Open, then click Open
          in the dialog. This works on macOS 14. On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later Apple removed the
          Control-click bypass, so do this instead: double-click the app once (it is refused), then
          open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click "Open Anyway" next to
          IslandPup, and confirm with the login password or Touch ID.
   c. A copy that arrived without a browser or AirDrop download (USB stick, same-network copy
      with scp or rsync) has no quarantine flag and opens normally.
   d. IslandPup is a menu bar app (no Dock icon). Look for the small dog at the top right.
   e. Permissions (Accessibility, Screen Recording, Camera, Microphone, ...) are asked per feature
      the first time it is used. Because the signature is ad-hoc, macOS ties each grant to the exact
      binary: after installing a NEW build, switch the app off and on again in System Settings >
      Privacy & Security > Accessibility (and Screen Recording) or remove it with "-" and add it
      again, otherwise System Settings shows it as allowed while the app is not trusted.
   f. Remove it: quit IslandPup, drag IslandPup.app to the Trash. Settings live in
      ~/Library/Preferences/local.islandpup.app.plist and ~/Library/Application Support/local.islandpup.app

4. Not verified by BRAND (say so in the README, do not claim it works)
   - Opening the zip on a second Mac: no second Mac was available. The steps above are standard
     macOS behaviour, not observed on this build.
   - Runtime behaviour of the built app: it was deliberately not launched.
